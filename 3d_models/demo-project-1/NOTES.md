# Design notes

- `base.kcl` and `lid.kcl` are separate editable enclosure parts; `main.kcl` positions the lid in the seated assembly position.
- The two cable openings are centered on the opposite 75 mm-long side walls, with their lower edges 2 mm above the inner floor.
- The specified lip inset leaves a 0.2 mm per-side nominal fit clearance inside the 96 x 71 mm base cavity. A 1.2 mm lip wall was selected as a practical editable default because its thickness was not specified.
